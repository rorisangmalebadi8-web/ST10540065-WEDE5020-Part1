About Reeh Flex

Our History

Reeh Flex started from a passion for fashion and creativity, with the goal of turning personal style into a brand that people could connect with. It has grown into a streetwear label focused on authenticity and standing out.

Our Mission

To create unique, quality streetwear that gives people the confidence to express themselves and own their style.

Our Vision

To grow Reeh Flex into a recognised South African streetwear brand with a strong international presence.

Who We Dress

Our target audience is young people, particularly Gen Z and young adults, who are passionate about streetwear, fashion, music, culture, and self-expression.
